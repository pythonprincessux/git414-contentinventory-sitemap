# Site Map Template

## Site Purpose
Provide clear and easy to read information on garden hours, location, programs, and volunteer opportunities for the campus and local community members.

## Primary Audience
Students, faculty, staff, and local community members who would like to visit, volunteer, or even attend the garden workshops.

## Primary User Tasks
1. Check garden hours, location, and accessibility before visiting.
2. Sign up to volunteer or attend an upcoming workshop.
3. Learn how to rent a plot to plant or donate their own fresh produce.

## Proposed Site Map

- Home
  - Purpose: Welcomes the visitors and provides them with quick links to hours, location, and current announcements.
  - Key content: Welcome message, hours of operation, garden address, and a few quick links to get involved.

- Visit
  - Purpose: This page will help visitors plan an in-person visit with directions and site details.
  - Key content: Operating days/hours, parking rules, public transit options, and accessibility features.

- Programs & Workshops
  - Purpose: This page informs visitors about oppurtunities to learn about gardening and garden activities.
  - Key content: Displaying workshop details, seasonal event schedules, and rental rules for plots.

- Get Involved
  - Purpose: This page explains how members of the community are able to contribute time, harvest, or resources.
  - Key content: Volunteer workdays, interest sign up form, and produce donation drop off instructions and guidelines.

- Contact
  - Purpose: This page gives visitors a way to ask questions or reach garden organizers.
  - Key content: Contact email, social links, and frequently asked questions.

## Content Risks to Resolve

1. Confirm the exact opening and closing hours for public visits instead of only stating "mornings."
2. Resolve and clarify the composting workshop start time conflict between 9:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
3. Make more specific procedures and hours for produce donation dropoffs.
