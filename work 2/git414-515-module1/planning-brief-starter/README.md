# Planning Content Architecture Starter

Use this starter for `1 / Practice: Content Inventory and Site Map`.

## Scenario

The Polytechnic Community Garden wants a small public website. The garden supports students, staff, and nearby residents who want to learn about garden hours, volunteer days, workshops, produce donation days, and accessibility information.

The site must stay within HTML/CSS scope. Do not plan account login, dynamic calendars, payment, or JavaScript-dependent filtering.

## Possible Pages

- Home
- About the Garden
- Volunteer Days
- Workshops
- Visit and Accessibility
- Contact or Interest Intake Form

## Raw Content Notes

- Garden is open Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday mornings.
- Volunteer days happen only twice per month. Days are still pending.
- Multiple workshops available. Workshops include seed starting, compost basics, water-wise planting, and accessible garden tools.
- The garden has raised beds and shaded seating so far.
- Parking and transit information need confirmation and more information.
- There are photos, but licensing and alt text are not ready. Site is not ready to be deployed live.
- Contact form should collect name, email, area of interest, and access needs. No unnecessary or sensitive information is needed to be exposed.
- Old flyer says workshops start at 9:00 AM, but a newer note says 10:00 AM. Note to self: confirm finalized hours.
- Donation produce is distributed through a campus pantry partner. Make sure info is included and clear,

## Your Practice Goal

Turn the raw notes into:

1. a content inventory;
2. a site map; and
3. one sentence explaining the purpose of each page.
